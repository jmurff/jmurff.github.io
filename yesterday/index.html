Yesterday

Today's weather, compared with a day you remember.

Coming soon, maybe

You know what yesterday felt like. Yesterday tells you how today is different, in one sentence: warmer or colder, windier or calmer, and what you wore the last time it was like this.

What it does

One sentence

The app opens to how today compares with yesterday, with the feels-like, high and low, wind and rain side by side below.

It learns you

Tap too cold, just right or too warm at the end of a day, and name what you wore. It learns your comfort range and speaks in your terms.

At a glance

Widgets for the Home Screen and Lock Screen, and complications for Apple Watch.

Your places

Follow where you are, or keep a few cities to compare.
