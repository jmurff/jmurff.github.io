Play it your way

CPU or friends

Play the CPU, with a choice of level in several games, or pass the phone around the table in many of them.

Head to head

Play thirteen of the board games against a friend on two iPhones, iPads or Macs in the same room. Turn it on in Settings, host a game, and your friend joins with the code on your screen.

Made for a folding phone

Open a folding phone such as iPhone Duo and the board sits on one half, with your hand and buttons on the other. On iPad and Mac the window splits the same way when it is wide enough.

Pick up where you left off

Games save as you play. Every game has its rules written out, and the play-by-play explains how each score or move came about.

Private by design

No account to create, no ads and no tracking. Stats can sync through your own iCloud, Game Center is optional, and head to head goes straight between the two devices. Read the Privacy Policy.
