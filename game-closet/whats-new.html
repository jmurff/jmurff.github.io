Game Closet: What's new

Release notes and version history.

Back to Game Closet

Version 1.5.0

Available on the App Store · Updated November 23, 2026

  • Head to head: ask to take back your last move, and the other player can allow it or say no.
  • Checkers has a Forced captures option. On, a side that can jump must jump, as the official rules say. Off, jumping is your choice, as many people play at home.
  • Play nearby explains that guest and public Wi-Fi can keep devices apart, and how to use a phone's hotspot instead.
  • To play each other head to head, both of you need this version.

Version 1.4.0

Available on the App Store · Updated November 20, 2026

  • Peg Triangle numbers every tee and hole, or names its place on the cross, and shows the hint in the status line.
  • Head to head checks that both players' versions can play together and says so before connecting.
  • Send Feedback in Settings opens an email with the app's version and your device filled in, and What's New in Settings shows what changed.
  • Fixes: turning a phone brings back the right layout, and Pachisi's and Cribbage's CPU explanations match the throw and the dealer.

Version 1.3.0

Available on the App Store · Updated November 17, 2026

  • Mahjong, Halma and Chicken Foot join the closet, with more variants for Backgammon, Yacht Dice, Gomoku, Morris and Hnefatafl.
  • Head to head now covers 39 games. Add CPU players to games for more than two, including CPU partners in partnership games.
  • Dice tumble across the felt, a Back to games button appears when a game ends, and Settings has an optional tip jar.
