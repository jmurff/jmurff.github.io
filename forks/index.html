Forks

A family recipe tree.

Coming soon, maybe

Grandma's chili is the root. Mom made it with less salt, and your brother made his from Mom's with chipotle. Forks keeps every version of a family dish and shows exactly what each cook changed.

What it does

Every version kept

Each version comes from another, so a dish grows into a tree you can read from Grandma's original to the newest twist.

What changed

See each version against the one it came from, or any other: what went in, what came out, and what changed amount.

The family favorite

Crown the version everyone asks for, so it's the one that opens first.

Start from a card

Scan an old handwritten recipe card to start a dish.
