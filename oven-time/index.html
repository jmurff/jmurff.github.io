Oven Time

Every dish ready at the same time.

Coming soon, maybe

Pick the dishes and when you want to eat. Oven Time works backward to schedule every step, keeps each oven at one temperature at a time, and walks you through the cooking as it happens.

What it does

Plans backward

Say when you want to eat, and every preheat, prep and bake gets its time, and each oven holds one temperature at a time.

Conducts the kitchen

Steps move toward a now line, one lane per cook, with big Start and Done buttons.

Keeps up with you

Alerts arrive even with the app closed, and running late replans everything after the late step.

On your wrist

Follow the next step on Apple Watch while your hands are busy.

Oven Time isn't out yet, and plans can change. Back to DeeDogDevelopment.
