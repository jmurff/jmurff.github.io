Scorekeeper

Pitch by pitch, play by play.

Coming soon to the App Store

Keep score the way a paper scorebook does, without the eraser. Scorekeeper scores baseball and softball pitch by pitch, cricket ball by ball, and basketball, football, soccer and hockey play by play, with a box score for every player. Scoring is free, with no limit on games.

See it in action

Score any game

Baseball and softball

Tap each pitch, then the play and the fielders (6-4-3). The count, outs and runners follow, and the scorecard fills in the way you'd draw it.

Cricket

Every ball, extras, wickets, bowling changes and the follow-on, checked against the Laws.

Basketball, football, soccer and hockey

Scores, fouls, shots and assists for each player, drawn on the court, field or rink.

Fix anything

Undo the last thing, or tap any earlier play to change it. The box score and play-by-play follow along.

Pitch counts for youth baseball

Pick your league's rules (Little League, Pitch Smart, or your own daily limit) and the age group. Scorekeeper warns before a pitcher reaches the limit and shows who needs rest, counting the team's earlier games. On Apple Watch, count pitches from the dugout: Ball, Strike, Foul and In Play, with how many are left before the limit.

Made for the fold

Half open on a bleacher, the scoreboard and field sit above the fold and the scoring buttons below it, under your thumbs. Open flat, the scorecard is on one side and the field on the other. Closed, it's a big scoreboard on the cover screen.

On iPad and Mac

It works just as well on any iPhone, iPad and Mac. On a big screen the scorecard sits right beside the field.

Your teams

Every team keeps its colors, logo, roster and record. Pick from 32 mascot logos, and each sport has its own scoreboard theme. Every game you score is kept in History.

Free and Pro

Free

No limit on games

  • Scoring all six sports, with the box score and play-by-play
  • Fixing any past play
  • Every pitch-count warning
  • Teams, rosters and History
  • Following a game someone shares with you
  • Joining a game nearby to score together
  • The Apple Watch pitch counter on its own

Scorekeeper Pro

One purchase, not a subscription

  • iCloud sync between your iPhone, iPad and Mac
  • Share Live: family follows the game as you score it, for free
  • Score Together: hand scoring back and forth with a phone nearby, even with no signal
  • The Apple Watch pitch counter, linked to the game you're scoring
  • Live scores: today's games and scores in the pro leagues, and each team's schedule
  • Season stats across your games, and a printable scorecard PDF
  • Lineups read from a photo of the lineup card, logos from your own pictures, and every theme

Buy Pro once and it's unlocked on your iPhone, iPad and Mac, and for your Family Sharing group. Once you have Pro, an optional tip jar appears in Settings.

No accounts, no ads

No sign-up, no ads and no tracking. Your games stay on your devices and in your own iCloud. Read the Privacy Policy.
