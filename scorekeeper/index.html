Scorekeeper

Keep score of any game, play by play.

Coming soon, maybe

Score baseball pitch by pitch, cricket ball by ball, and basketball, football, soccer and hockey play by play, with a box score for each player. Or follow a live MLB game and watch the scorecard fill itself in.

Open it up

On iPhone Duo, open the phone and the game spreads across both halves: the box score and plays on one, the field and the scoring buttons on the other.

What it does

Six sports, one scorebook

Baseball and softball, cricket, basketball, football, soccer and hockey, each with its own field, buttons and rules written out.

Every play counted

Mark where each shot was taken or each hit went. Box scores, shot charts and the play-by-play build themselves, and a past play can be fixed.

Follow live games

Follow an MLB game and watch its scorecard fill in, or follow NBA, WNBA, NFL, NHL, MLS and college games as they happen.

Teams and history

Every game you score is kept, with each team's record. Share a game's scorebook as a PDF, or send the game itself to a friend.
