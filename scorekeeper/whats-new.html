Scorekeeper: What's new

Release notes and version history.

Back to Scorekeeper

Version 1.3.0

Available on the App Store · Updated November 23, 2026

  • Share Live can now send a link. Anyone who opens it can follow the game in a web browser, with no app or account: the score, the situation, the line score and the last plays. A link ends when you stop it, or 48 hours after its last update.
  • A followed tennis match draws the court, with each player at their end and the server in the court they serve from.
  • Tap a row in a list to highlight its mark on the full-size field, and every court, field, pitch and rink keeps the same minimum height, so it stays readable on the cover display and smaller phones.
  • Score Together and Join a Game Nearby explain that guest Wi-Fi can keep phones apart, and how to use a hotspot instead.

Version 1.2.0

Available on the App Store · Updated November 20, 2026

  • Follow ATP and WTA tennis on the Live tab, with set scores, tiebreaks, retirements, walkovers and match statistics.
  • Football adds up each play's yards and asks whether it was a run, pass, sack or incomplete, and who made it. Box scores add Passing, Rushing, Receiving and Defense tables, and drives list the latest first.
  • Baseball's pad takes a triple play or any other play under a code you type, and followed basketball games count blocked shots.
  • Send Feedback in Settings opens an email with your version and device filled in, About shows the version and build, and What's New in Settings shows what changed.

Version 1.1.0

Available on the App Store · Updated November 15, 2026

  • Volleyball, pickleball and tennis join the free scoring tools, with rotations and libero tracking, singles and doubles, and sport-specific scoring.
  • Football adds penalty yardage, run and pass tracking, and expanded team stats.
  • Scorekeeper Live adds a monthly or yearly subscription that includes Pro. Follow supported pro and college games play by play, with scorecards and box scores that fill in as games are played.
  • The Live tab adds All, Pro and College filters, team search, and improved refresh and final-game status.
