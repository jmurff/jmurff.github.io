Fork&Oven Support

Email support@deedogdevelopment.com or open Settings in Fork&Oven and tap Support under About. For a bug report, include your device, system version, app version, and what happened.

Save or share a family recipe

Open Recipes and tap New Dish. Enter the recipe or choose Import Card Photo to read a handwritten card. Review the ingredients and steps before you save. Open a saved version and use Fork This to make your own version or Share Recipe to send a copy.

Import or write a recipe for a meal

In Meal, open Add Dishes and tap Import Recipe to fetch a web recipe or open a recipe file. If a web page can’t be imported, use New Recipe to enter it by hand. Check the step durations, temperatures, and equipment before you add it to a meal.

To import from Safari, tap Add Safari Shortcut in the app, then Add Shortcut in Shortcuts. In Safari, choose Share, then Import into Fork&Oven. To move recipes from Paprika, export them in Paprika Recipe Format and choose the .paprikarecipes file.

Build a meal plan

In Meal, set Serve at, choose your dishes, and set up the cooks and equipment you have. Tap Build the Plan to see the schedule. Review the steps, then tap Start Cooking when you’re ready.

A hands-on step takes a cook’s time, like chopping or stirring. A passive step, like baking, runs while that cook works on something else. Touch and hold a step on the timeline to move it earlier or later; Reset Moves undoes your changes. Layout switches between the timeline, the Dial, and the Kitchen. If you add a recipe while a plan is open, tap Rebuild Plan to include it.

Cooking the meal

Tap Start and Done on each step, and It’s Hot when the oven reaches temperature. Glance Mode shows what’s next in large type. The next steps also show on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island. For long roasts, Fork&Oven can set an alarm that rings even when your phone is silenced; allow alarms when it asks, or later in your device’s Settings.

Cook with Apple Watch

Open the Fork&Oven companion on your paired Apple Watch during a cooking session started on your iPhone. If the Watch is waiting for a meal you already started, open Fork&Oven on the iPhone and check that the Watch is connected.

Saved plans and iCloud

Meals you build appear under Plans by serving date. When you’re signed in to iCloud, saved plans go to your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. If a plan doesn’t show on another device, check that both are signed in with the same Apple Account, open Fork&Oven on both, and pull down on Plans to sync again.
