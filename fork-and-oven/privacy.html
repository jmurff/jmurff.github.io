Fork&Oven Privacy Policy

Last updated November 22, 2026.

Fork&Oven saves your recipes, meal plans, and cooking history on your device, and your saved plans in your own iCloud. The app requires no account and has no ads, analytics, or tracking. DeeDogDevelopment does not receive your recipes or meal plans from the app.

Recipes and meals on your device

Fork&Oven stores recipes, family recipe versions, saved recipe card photos, meal plans, and cooking history locally. During a meal, it can send cooking steps and progress to your paired Apple Watch. Changes you make on the Watch can go back to the paired iPhone.

Saved plans in iCloud (Apple)

When you are signed in to iCloud, Fork&Oven keeps the meals you save under Plans in your private iCloud database, so they follow you to your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account. Apple stores and protects this data under your Apple Account and Apple’s privacy policy. DeeDogDevelopment cannot see it. To remove it, delete the plans in the app or remove Fork&Oven’s data in your device’s iCloud storage settings.

Importing recipes and recipe cards

When you import a recipe from the web, your device connects directly to the website you choose. The website receives a web request from your device and handles it under its own privacy policy.

When you choose a photo of a recipe card, Fork&Oven reads the selected image on your device and can save a copy with the recipe.

Sharing a recipe

When you share a recipe, Apple’s Share Sheet sends a copy to the app or recipient you choose. The recipient controls their copy. The services you choose for sharing or saving a copy have their own privacy policies.

Tips

Fork&Oven is free. If you choose to leave a tip, it is an in-app purchase handled by Apple through the App Store, under Apple’s privacy policy. DeeDogDevelopment never sees your payment details.

Support and feedback

The app opens an email draft with your app version when you choose Support or Send Feedback. You decide what to include and whether to send it. If you send it, DeeDogDevelopment receives your email address and the information in your message and uses them to respond to your request.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@deedogdevelopment.com.
