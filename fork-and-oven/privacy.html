Fork&Oven Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026.

Fork&Oven keeps your recipes, notes, photos, meal plans, and cooking history on your device and, if you choose, in your own private iCloud. The app requires no account and has no ads, analytics, or tracking. DeeDogDevelopment does not receive your recipes, photos, or meal plans from the app.

Recipes and meals on your device

Fork&Oven stores your recipes, family recipe versions, notes, photos, star ratings, favorites, saved plans, and cooking history on your device. During a meal, it can send cooking steps and progress to your paired Apple Watch. Changes you make on the Watch can go back to the paired iPhone.

Sync with iCloud (Apple)

With Sync With iCloud on and your device signed in to iCloud, Fork&Oven also keeps your recipes, recipe versions, notes, photos, ratings, favorites, saved plans, and cooking history in your private iCloud database, so each of your devices signed in to the same Apple Account has the same ones. Apple stores and protects this data under your Apple Account and Apple’s privacy policy. DeeDogDevelopment cannot see it.

You can turn sync off in Fork&Oven’s Settings; what is already on your device stays there. To remove the synced copy, delete the items in the app or remove Fork&Oven’s data in your device’s iCloud storage settings.

Backups

When you back up in Settings, Fork&Oven saves a backup file with your recipes, plans, photos, and cooking history to your iCloud Drive, or on your device when iCloud Drive is off. A backup stays under your control: you can restore it, share it with the app or person you choose, or delete it. DeeDogDevelopment does not receive your backups.

Photos and the camera

When you add a photo to a recipe or choose a photo of a recipe card, Fork&Oven reads only the photo you select and saves a smaller copy with the recipe. It does not keep the photo’s location. Fork&Oven uses the camera only when you take a photo for a recipe.

Importing recipes

When you import a recipe from the web, your device connects directly to the website you choose. The website receives a web request from your device and handles it under its own privacy policy.

Sharing recipes and plans

When you share a recipe, a plan, or a backup, Apple’s Share Sheet sends a copy to the app or recipient you choose. The recipient controls their copy. The services you choose for sharing or saving a copy have their own privacy policies.

Tips

Fork&Oven is free. If you choose to leave a tip, it is an in-app purchase handled by Apple through the App Store, under Apple’s privacy policy. DeeDogDevelopment never sees your payment details.

Support and feedback

The app opens an email draft with your app version when you choose Support or Send Feedback. You decide what to include and whether to send it. If you send it, DeeDogDevelopment receives your email address and the information in your message and uses them to respond to your request.

Contact

For privacy questions, email support@deedogdevelopment.com.
