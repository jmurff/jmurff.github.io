Fork&Oven

Your family recipes, with a plan to get dinner on the table.

Coming Soon

Keep Grandma’s original chili and the version you make with black beans. When it’s time to cook, choose the recipes and your serving time. Fork&Oven works backward to figure out when to start each step.

In testing for iPhone and iPad, with an Apple Watch companion.

What it does

Keep every version

Start with a handwritten recipe card or type it in. Make your own version, see what changed, and keep the original in the same family recipe tree.

Bring your own recipes

Import a recipe from a website or a file, or write your own. Review the ingredients and cooking steps before you add it to a meal.

Share a recipe copy

Send a version through the Share Sheet so someone else can cook it or save a copy.

Plan backward from dinner

Set a serving time, choose the dishes, and tell Fork&Oven how many cooks, ovens, and burners you have. Follow the cooking steps on your device or on a paired Apple Watch.
