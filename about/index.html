About Jim Murff

I make DeeDogDevelopment's apps. I've written software for more than 30 years and built iOS apps since 2011.

Before DeeDogDevelopment I spent six years at Dexcom as a senior iOS engineer on the G7 and Stelo continuous glucose monitoring apps. Before that I led iOS work on theme park and resort apps at accesso, and built apps for Petnet's smart pet feeder, PEAR Sports' training coach and ScrollMotion's enterprise platform.

I started out as a network administrator in the U.S. Air Force, then spent two decades on Unix systems and networking at Intel, McAfee and several San Diego startups. I live in Encinitas, California.
