Release notes and version history.

Back to TallyUp Golf

Version 1.5.0

Available on the App Store · Updated November 15, 2026

  • Follow PGA Tour players through the season, with standings, stats and events. Tour scorecards add strokes gained, driving, greens and short game stats.
  • Share a scorecard as a PDF or send a round to a friend with TallyUp Golf. Courses, favorites, maps, elevation and followed players sync through iCloud.
  • Continue Round brings back a round finished early, and finished scorecards show how many holes were played and when the last score went in.
  • Score buttons fit better on smaller iPhones, with fixes for side game settlement, iCloud sync and VoiceOver.
