TallyUp Golf: What's new

Release notes and version history.

Back to TallyUp Golf

Version 1.9.0

Available on the App Store · Updated November 25, 2026

  • Course search now goes around the world: more than 18,000 courses outside the United States, from Canada, the UK and Ireland across Europe to Japan, Korea, Australia and New Zealand, from OpenStreetMap.
  • Where a course's holes are mapped, its card comes with each hole's par and handicap, and yardages from each set of tees. Where they aren't, the holes start at par 4 and TallyUp tells you which ones to fill in before you tee off.
  • Find a course by its name in English or in its own language, by its town, or with its country's code: “kawana” finds 川奈ホテルゴルフコース, and “carnoustie gb” finds every course in Carnoustie.
  • Each course shows its town, or its suburb in a big city, and its country.
  • Settings now opens from the gear on every tab.

Version 1.8.0

Available on the App Store · Updated November 24, 2026

  • Once a week on Wi-Fi, your Library checks each course for a newer map and scorecard. A course that changed says Updated, with the date, and the holes you marked stay as they are.
  • Send a course to a friend from your Library, with its map, elevation and the holes you marked. It opens in their Library, ready to play.
  • The side view's elevation now reaches courses outside the United States too, from Open-Elevation's coarser satellite data.
  • Course search answers from a copy of every US course kept on TallyUp's own server, so it no longer waits on the course directory.
  • When the course directory is missing some of a course's pars, those holes start at par 4 and TallyUp tells you which ones to fix before you tee off.

Version 1.7.0

Available on the App Store · Updated November 23, 2026

  • Every stat list now lines up its values and ranks in their own columns, so they're easy to read down and compare.

Version 1.6.0

Available on the App Store · Updated November 20, 2026

  • Send Feedback in Settings opens an email to support with the app's version, your device and system, and where iCloud sync stands already filled in.
  • What's New in Settings shows what changed in this version, with a link to this version history.

Version 1.5.0

Available on the App Store · Updated November 15, 2026

  • Follow PGA Tour players through the season, with standings, stats and events. Tour scorecards add strokes gained, driving, greens and short game stats.
  • Share a scorecard as a PDF or send a round to a friend with TallyUp Golf. Courses, favorites, maps, elevation and followed players sync through iCloud.
  • Continue Round brings back a round finished early, and finished scorecards show how many holes were played and when the last score went in.
  • Score buttons fit better on smaller iPhones, with fixes for side game settlement, iCloud sync and VoiceOver.
