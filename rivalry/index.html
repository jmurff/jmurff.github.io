Rivalry

Who's really better? Now you'll know.

Coming soon, maybe

Ping-pong, cribbage, kart racing, backyard games: log who won, and Rivalry keeps the lifetime record for everyone you play with. Ratings, a title belt that changes hands, and a rivalry card to share.

What it does

A title belt

Each game has a belt. The only way to take it is to beat whoever holds it, and every defense counts.

Fair ratings

Every match updates each player's rating for that game, so a win over the best player counts for more.

Head to head

Your record against each person, your streaks, your nemesis, and the one you always beat. Share it as a card.

Scorekeeper games count

Games finished in Scorekeeper come in on their own, so you don't log them twice. For iPhone and Apple Watch.

Rivalry isn't out yet, and plans can change. Back to DeeDogDevelopment.
